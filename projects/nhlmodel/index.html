NHL Prediction Model

A Python pipeline for pregame NHL prediction, combining game statistics, shot quality, goalie, injury, and betting-market data.

I built ingestion and validation workflows, organized the data into Parquet layers, and aligned 13,117 historical moneyline rows to NHL game IDs. Models were evaluated chronologically, with feature families retained only when they improved validation log loss.

The selected model used market odds and team context. On the 2024–25 holdout, its log loss was 0.6612 versus 0.6578 for the market, and AUC was 0.6260 versus 0.6346. The market performed better on both measures.
