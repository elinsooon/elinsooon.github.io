Spatial SIR Model with Individual Mobility

A joint APM348 project with Ethan Kharitonov, extending a stochastic SIR epidemic model with individual movement between shared hubs, represented by Markov chains.

I contributed to the model formulation, state-space lumping analysis, and Python simulation code. I ran Monte Carlo experiments and analyzed outbreak-size distributions and sensitivity to initial conditions.

The simulations showed how hub-specific transmission and movement patterns affect final outbreak sizes, with substantial variation between runs. We developed a quotient-chain formulation, but direct computation remained impractical, so the experiments used simulation. The study used synthetic movement patterns.
