Toronto Traffic Heatmap

An animated map of daytime Toronto movement built from turning movement counts and an OpenStreetMap road graph. Particles show activity across modes, while road colors show relative car volume.

I processed intersection observations into directed segment flows and hourly route pools. The browser uses MapLibre and deck.gl, with a Web Worker loading binary route data. The processed network includes 3,987 count locations and 43,763 directed segments.

The map covers 7 AM to 7 PM. Routes and particle speeds are generated for the visualization; the counts do not reconstruct individual journeys or measured congestion. The app’s “Transit” category combines buses and trucks.
